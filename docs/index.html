Tam analizi gor
Gercek Ornek

TR Durumu
Turkey possesses a strong manufacturing base, but many SMEs struggle with the high initial cost and complexity of automation, hindering global competitiveness.
TR Uygulama Fikri
Develop and deploy easy-to-configure, modular robotic systems specifically designed for the needs of Turkish small to medium-sized manufacturers, integrating AI for adaptive task execution, quality control, and predictive maintenance to drive cost savings and efficiency. This enhances local production capabilities.
Ilk MVP
A flexible robotic arm system capable of performing pick-and-place or simple assembly tasks, controlled via a user-friendly interface, with AI-powered vision for quality checks.
Hedef Musteri
Small and medium-sized manufacturers in automotive, textiles, food processing, and general assembly sectors in Turkey.
Gelir Modeli
Hardware sales of modular robotic units, SaaS subscription for software/AI features, maintenance and support contracts.
Neden Simdi
Strong manufacturing base combined with the need for increased efficiency and competitiveness, driven by high inflation, makes affordable automation solutions critical.
Riskler
High upfront R&D costs, competition from established global robot manufacturers, and educating SME owners on automation benefits.

Gercek Ornek

TR Durumu
Turkey's startup ecosystem is maturing, but employee equity remains illiquid, especially with high inflation eroding value over time.
TR Uygulama Fikri
Create a platform providing integrated equity management tools for Turkish startups (ESOPs, cap table management) combined with a controlled secondary marketplace for employees to sell a portion of their vested shares, compliant with local regulations. Riskler
