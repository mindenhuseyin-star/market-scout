Tam analizi gor
Gercek Ornek
Used car bidding startup - pits dealerships against each other to bid on your used car.
TR Durumu
Turkey has a large and active used car market, but sellers often struggle to get fair prices and trust is a significant issue; e-commerce is growing rapidly.
TR Uygulama Fikri
Create a mobile-first online platform where individual car owners can list their used vehicles, and registered Turkish dealerships can compete in a transparent bidding process to purchase the car, ensuring sellers get the best market value and a hassle-free experience.
Ilk MVP
A mobile app where users can upload car details and photos, schedule an inspection, and then receive real-time bids from a network of pre-vetted dealerships within a set timeframe.
Hedef Musteri
Individual car sellers and licensed used car dealerships across Turkey.
Gelir Modeli
Commission on successful sales (from the dealer), premium features for dealers (e.g., lead generation, analytics).
Neden Simdi
Turkey's 30%/yr e-commerce growth and mobile-first population make a digital, transparent solution highly attractive for a high-value transaction like used cars.
Riskler
Building trust with both sellers and dealers, managing logistics of inspections and transfers, and intense competition from existing classifieds.

Cybersecurity/Mobile App/AI
Firsat Skoru█████████░ 87%
Kaynak: Savi
Tam analizi gor
Gercek Ornek
Savi's app - aims to protect consumers from realistic AI scams like kidnappers demanding ransom.
TR Durumu
Turkey is a mobile-first country with a large internet user base, making its citizens vulnerable to increasingly sophisticated AI-generated scams (voice cloning, deepfakes, phishing).
TR Uygulama Fikri
Develop a mobile application specifically for the Turkish market that uses AI to detect and warn users about realistic AI-powered scams, such as voice cloning for ransom demands, deepfake video calls, or sophisticated phishing attempts, with culturally specific warnings and guidance.
Ilk MVP
A mobile app that integrates with phone calls and messaging, analyzing incoming communications for suspicious AI-generated elements and providing real-time alerts and educational content on common scam types in Turkey.
Hedef Musteri
General mobile users in Turkey, particularly vulnerable groups like the elderly or those less digitally literate.
Gelir Modeli
Freemium model with basic protection free, premium features (e.g., family protection, advanced threat intelligence, direct support) on subscription.
Neden Simdi
The increasing sophistication of AI scams globally, combined with Turkey's mobile-first population and varied digital literacy, creates an urgent need for such a protective solution.
Riskler
High false-positive rate, maintaining accuracy against evolving scam techniques, user adoption and integration with phone systems.
