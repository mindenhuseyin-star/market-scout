Komşu Pazar (Neighbor Market)
Social commerce with integrated P2P payments for local goods.
E-commerce, FinTech, Social Media
Firsat Skoru██████████ 95%
Kaynak: TikTok P2P payments, Perplexity's freemium in India
Tam analizi gor
Gercek Ornek
TikTok exploring peer-to-peer payments via DMs.
TR Durumu
Turkey is mobile-first with rapidly growing e-commerce and a strong social media presence; local markets are popular.
TR Uygulama Fikri
Create a mobile-first platform where users can discover and purchase goods directly from local producers, artisans, and small businesses through interactive social feeds. Integrate secure in-app P2P payment and local delivery options.
Ilk MVP
A local marketplace app for a specific city/district, allowing vendors to post products and users to chat/pay securely for pickup/delivery.
Hedef Musteri
Small local businesses, artisans, farmers, and consumers seeking unique local products.
Gelir Modeli
Transaction fees, premium vendor features, localized advertising.
Neden Simdi
Exploding e-commerce, mobile penetration, and the desire for authentic local products combine with P2P payment convenience.
Riskler
Building trust in P2P payments, managing quality control for diverse products, competition from established e-commerce giants.
