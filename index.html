Supply Chain Tech, SaaS
Firsat Skoru█████████░ 87%
Kaynak: Veritas
Tam analizi gor
Gercek Ornek
Veritas - anti-counterfeit chip for jewelry, trophies, fragrances.
TR Durumu
Turkish manufacturers face significant challenges with counterfeiting both domestically and internationally, impacting brand reputation and revenue. E-commerce growth exacerbates this.
TR Uygulama Fikri
Develop an integrated solution combining unique, verifiable digital identifiers (e.g., NFC tags, QR codes with blockchain-backed authenticity) for products with a consumer-facing app to check authenticity and track product origin. Target high-value exports like textiles, food, and jewelry.
Ilk MVP
NFC/QR code generation and a simple mobile app for consumers to scan and verify authenticity of a single product category (e.g., specific brand of olive oil or luxury textile).
Hedef Musteri
Export-oriented Turkish manufacturers, luxury goods brands, and food producers.
Gelir Modeli
Per-unit tag/identifier cost + SaaS subscription for the traceability platform and analytics.
Neden Simdi
Growing e-commerce makes digital verification crucial, while international trade demands higher transparency and authenticity, giving Turkish brands a competitive edge.
Riskler
Cost of implementation for manufacturers, consumer adoption of verification tools, integration with existing supply chain systems.
